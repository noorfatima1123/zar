Meet the Editor.

ZAR Consultancy is pleased to announce exclusive Meet the Editor sessions with leading journal editors and academic experts. Each session is one hour on Zoom, followed by discussion on the practical questions researchers face most often when preparing and submitting manuscripts.

Co-Editor-in-Chief (ijMPB) & Associate Professor, Project Studies
Dr. Francesco Di Maddaloni
The Bartlett, UCL
Date 3rd December 2025
Time 5:00 PM (Dubai Time)
Mode Zoom · 1 hour

Discussion Points

  • How to select the right journal?
  • How to pass the initial editorial screening?
  • Common reasons for rejection.
  • Legitimate and ethical use of AI in research.

This session will guide researchers on improving their manuscripts and increasing their chances of journal acceptance.

Co-Founder & Editor-in-Chief, Review of Behavioral Economics · Former Editor-in-Chief, Journal of Socio-Economics (Elsevier) · Dean & Chair, Behavioral & Institutional Economics
Professor Morris Altman
University of Dundee
Date 17th December 2025
Time 1:00 PM (UK) · 5:00 PM (Dubai)
Mode Zoom · 1 hour

Discussion Points

  • How to select the journal?
  • How to pass the initial screening?
  • What are the main reasons for rejection?
  • What is the legitimate use of AI?
  • How to address sample size and its significance in empirical papers?

This session will help researchers select the right journal, avoid common rejections, use AI appropriately, and handle sample size effectively to boost manuscript acceptance.
