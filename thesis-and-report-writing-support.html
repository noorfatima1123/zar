Are you seeking expert guidance for your Master's Thesis or Senior Design Project? ZAR Consultancy is your trusted partner, offering customized guidance from research topic selection to final report.

  • Expert guidance at every stage — experienced professionals offer personalized support throughout your thesis or senior design project, ensuring high-quality, well-researched work
  • Timely delivery — deadlines matter; on-time delivery is guaranteed

  • Tailored support — services customized to the specific requirements of your academic program

Our Services Include

Topic Selection · Literature Review · Questionnaire designing / Google Survey Form · Data Collection · Data Analysis (using relevant statistical techniques) · Data Interpretation · Results and Discussion · References · Complete Report Writing · Complete Report Formatting (according to your institutional formatting guidelines) · Final Presentation

Research Topic Selection

  • Assistance in selecting unique, researchable topics
  • Refining research questions and objectives
  • Developing theoretical and conceptual frameworks
  • Support in crafting research proposals and designing instruments (surveys, questionnaires, interview guidelines)

Literature Review

  • Reviewing of relevant literature
  • Authentic in-text citations
  • Latest and credible referencing

Questionnaire Designing / Data Collection

  • Designing questionnaires
  • Design and setup of surveys (Google Forms, MS Forms)
  • Data collection strategies and response analysis
  • Transcription of interviews and survey responses

Data Analysis and Results

  • Data cleaning, preprocessing, and visualization
  • Qualitative & quantitative analysis (SPSS, R, Python, NVivo, Excel, etc.)
  • Hypothesis testing, regression, ANOVA, correlation
  • Coding and thematic analysis for qualitative research
  • Interpretation and discussion of results

Complete Report Writing

Report chapter structure:

  • Chap 1: Introduction
  • Chap 2: Literature Review
  • Chap 3: Research Methodology
  • Chap 4: Data Collection
  • Chap 5: Results and Discussion
  • Chap 6: Conclusion and Recommendation

Complete Report Formatting

  • Overall layout of the report
  • Grammar, spelling, and sentence structuring
  • Enhancing coherence, readability, and style consistency
  • Rewriting and paraphrasing to simplify content without changing meaning
  • Plagiarism checking and rewriting flagged content

References

  • Referencing (IEEE, APA, MLA, Chicago, etc.)
  • Accurate in-text citations and reference list formatting
  • Bibliography creation using citation tools (Zotero, EndNote, etc.)

Presentation

  • PowerPoint slides for thesis defense
  • Visuals like infographics, charts, and posters
  • Technical support with LaTeX, PDF formatting, tables, and appendices
