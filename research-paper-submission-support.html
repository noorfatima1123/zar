Overview

Taking the submission process off your desk.

Nowadays, submission of research papers has become a challenging activity for authors and researchers. The selection of the most suitable journal needs careful matching of the paper's theme and the journal scope. The next step is the conversion of the paper according to journal guidelines — each publisher and journal has their own formatting guidelines.

These activities need a lot of time and thorough understanding of the whole process. The research team of ZAR is fully trained to take care of this whole process for you.

Initial Review before Submission

A detailed review of the paper before official submission. If improvement is required, our experts provide a comprehensive list of suggestions covering title, literature review, research methodology, data analysis, interpretation of results, and overall contribution to the field — increasing chances of acceptance.

Editing & Proofreading

Comprehensive editing and proofreading to improve the content, structure and organization of the manuscript. This involves:

  • Content evaluation
  • Structural organization
  • Clarity and coherence
  • Consistency and layout
  • Enhancing readability
  • Grammar correction
  • In-text referencing

Plagiarism Check

The originality and novelty of the content is critical for successful publication. Our experts are professional in paraphrasing and re-writing information as per international guidelines to maintain the highest level of academic integrity.

Journal Selection

Choosing an appropriate journal plays an important role in timely publication. Many papers are instantly rejected by editors due to non-matching of the paper with the scope of the journal. Our experts are trained to find the most suitable journal.

Paper Submission

Successful submission involves many steps. Due to heavy workload, authors face difficulty completing the process in one sitting, causing unnecessary delays. The team of experts can take care of the whole submission process under the author's supervision.
