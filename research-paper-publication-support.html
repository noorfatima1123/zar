Our Services

Research Paper Publication Support

In this program, we facilitate faculty members and researchers in the publication of quality research papers.

See what's included
Overview

Publication support for researchers, end to end.

Despite having excellent research potential, many faculty members and researchers find their publications hampered by challenges like extensive teaching loads, administrative duties, language barriers, high article processing charges, or limited availability of labs.

We support a robust network of authors and researchers who complement each other for quality publications — and we work alongside you at every stage, from initial review through to submission, reviewer response, and resubmission if needed.

Initial Review before Submission

You will be provided a detailed review of your paper before its official submission. The objective is to improve the quality of your paper before formal submission. Our experts provide a comprehensive list of suggestions after reviewing each aspect of the paper — title, literature review, research methodology, data analysis, interpretation of results, and overall contribution to the field. This maximizes the chances of passing initial screening, reviewer acceptance, and subsequent publication.

Data Analysis

Results and findings are the backbone of every good research paper. Our team is adept at R, Stata and SPSS, and assists with:

  • Data organizing and cleaning
  • Descriptive statistics
  • Inferential statistics such as ANOVA, t-tests, regression analysis, correlation analysis, or other advanced statistical techniques
  • Interpretation of results
  • Drawing of graphs, tables and figures

Editing & Proofreading

Comprehensive editing and proofreading to improve the content, structure and organization of the manuscript. This involves:

  • Content evaluation
  • Structural organization
  • Clarity and coherence
  • Consistency and layout
  • Enhancing readability
  • Grammar correction
  • In-text referencing

Plagiarism Check

The originality and novelty of the content is critical for successful publication. Our experts are professional in paraphrasing and re-writing information as per international guidelines to maintain the highest level of academic integrity.

Journal Selection

Choosing an appropriate journal plays an important role in timely publication. Many papers are instantly rejected by editors due to non-matching of the paper with the scope of the journal. Our experts are trained to find the most suitable journal.

Formatting of Manuscript according to Journal Guidelines

  • Thoroughly review the paper submission guidelines
  • Format the paper according to them
  • Correct the in-text citation style
  • Organize the sections
  • Format headings and subheadings
  • Format figures and tables
  • Use the correct referencing style

Paper Submission

Successful submission involves many steps. Due to heavy workload, authors face difficulty completing the submission process in one sitting, causing unnecessary delays. Our team can guide professionally or take care of the whole submission process under the author's supervision.

Handling of Reviewers' Comments

After passing initial screening, one of the most critical tasks is addressing reviewer concerns and comments. This requires careful understanding of comments and subsequent improvements to the manuscript. Our team facilitates this whole process and modifies the paper to fulfil the requirements of the blind reviewing process.

Resubmission of Rejected Papers

If your paper is rejected, we support the entire resubmission process:

  • Analyzing the causes of rejection
  • Paper refinement in light of the reviewer's feedback
  • Structural and content adjustments
  • Selection of a new journal
  • Re-formatting and compliance with the new journal
  • Reviewing and final proofreading
  • Re-submission in the new journal

Organic Citation Booster (complimentary)

We boost citations of published papers by promoting them across ZAR's network of authors, researchers and professors. Over the last few years, this has enhanced the academic profile of ZAR's research collaborators.

  • Plagiarism check Included
  • Resubmission support Included
  • Citation booster Complimentary
