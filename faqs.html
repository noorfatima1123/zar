ZAR brings together four practices under one roof: academic management consultancies (research paper publication, reviewing, submission, thesis writing), digital promotion consultancy, training and development consultancies, and AI automation. Visit our Services section to explore each in detail.

We work with researchers, faculty members, universities, educational institutions, businesses, and professionals across the UAE and beyond. Our clientele includes both emerging and developed organizations as well as individuals aiming to excel in their fields.

ZAR is primarily located in Dubai, UAE, with representatives available in selected countries for in-person or Zoom meetings. While we're based in the UAE, we serve clients globally — including researchers, students, and organizations across the US, UK, Europe, Asia, and the Middle East. All of our remote services are available worldwide.

Our dedicated AMT members can facilitate you in the publication of quality research papers in Web of Science and Scopus indexed journals — from idea generation through to final publication.

We support you end-to-end: initial review before submission, data analysis (R, Stata, SPSS), editing and proofreading, plagiarism check, journal selection, formatting according to journal guidelines, the actual submission, and handling of reviewers' comments. We also support resubmission of rejected papers — plus a complimentary organic citation booster.

We only target Web of Science and Scopus indexed journals for the publication of research papers.

ZAR firmly believes in the policy of research integrity and publication ethics, and we strictly follow the defined timelines of the relevant publishers and journals. Timelines vary by journal and field — your AMT contact will walk you through what to expect for your specific paper.

ZAR firmly believes in the policy of genuine research and strictly follows the plagiarism guidelines issued by the relevant journals and publishers.

We have experts across various research domains who work with you to ensure the quality of your paper — from methodology and analysis through to language and structure.

AMT members facilitate you at every stage of the publication process, starting from idea generation all the way to final publication.
