How it works

Five steps from paper to final upload.

You give the expert opinion. We handle the drafting, the guidelines, and the details.

01

Discuss

You discuss the paper that comes to you for reviewing.

02

Draft

As per guidelines of the journal, relevant experts prepare a professional and detailed review of the paper.

03

Review

The first review is shared with you for your expert opinion.

04

Finalise

The final review is prepared based on your recommendations, if any.

05

Upload

You upload the final review document on the journal website.
