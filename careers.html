Why ZAR

Work that helps people move forward.

ZAR Management Consultancies works at the intersection of research, growth, and technology. Every project we take on helps a researcher publish, a business grow, an organisation develop its people, or a team put AI to work — and every role here contributes directly to that outcome.

We're a small, focused team. You'll work across disciplines, take ownership of real deliverables, and see the effect of your work quickly. If you care about doing work that matters — and want to keep learning across academic, digital, and AI domains — you'll fit right in.

What we offer

  • Fully remote work — every role is remote, so you can work from anywhere
  • Cross-disciplinary work — academic, digital, training, and AI, all under one roof
  • Direct ownership — real responsibility on real client work, from early on
  • Growth mindset — we invest in learning and in the tools you need to do your best work
  • Freshers welcome — many of our roles are open to candidates with 0–6 months of experience
  • Meaningful outcomes — every project contributes to someone's progress

Who we look for

We hire across a range of functions — research consulting, digital marketing, training and development, AI and automation engineering, and operations. All of our roles are open to candidates anywhere in the world.

How to Apply

A simple, three-step process.

1. Fill the application form

Click Apply for this role on any job card above. A short Google Form opens in a new tab — just fill in your details, attach or link your CV, and submit.

2. Introductory conversation

If your background looks like a fit, we'll schedule a short introductory call to talk through your experience, what you're looking for, and what the role involves.

3. Follow-up discussion

Depending on the role, we may ask you to complete a short practical exercise or take part in a second conversation with a relevant team lead — so we both get a clearer picture before making a decision.
